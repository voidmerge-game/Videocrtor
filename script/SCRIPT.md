## Fact-check notes
- "Ten percent / ninety percent" are the standard summary figures (Pakenham, *The Scramble for Africa*).
- Berlin Conference: 14 states incl. the USA and the Ottoman Empire; 15 Nov 1884 – 26 Feb 1885. The General Act did not formally award the Congo; recognition of the International Association came through bilateral agreements during the conference. VO phrasing ("the conference hands...") is the accepted shorthand; can be softened if you prefer strict wording.
- Maxim gun 1884; Belloc's couplet is from *The Modern Traveller* (1898).
- Omdurman (2 Sep 1898): ~10,000 Mahdists killed, 48 British/Egyptian force dead.
- Adwa (1 Mar 1896): Italian force ≈ 17,000; ≈ 6,000 Italians killed.
- Congo population decline: estimates range widely (Hochschild ≈ 10 million; others lower). VO says "fallen by millions" deliberately.
- Herero: ≈ 65,000 of ≈ 80,000 (≈ 80%) died; Nama ≈ 50%. Von Trotha order, 2 Oct 1904.
- Boer War camps: ≈ 26,000–28,000 Boer civilians (majority children) + ≈ 20,000 Black Africans in separate camps.
- "177 ethnic groups partitioned": Michalopoulos & Papaioannou (2016).
- Fashoda: 18 Sep 1898, Marchand (≈120 tirailleurs + 12 officers) met Kitchener; French withdrawal ordered 3 Nov 1898; Entente Cordiale 8 Apr 1904.
- Morocco: First Crisis 1905–06 (Tangier, Algeciras); Agadir Crisis 1911 (SMS Panther); Treaty of Fez 30 Mar 1912.
- OAU Cairo resolution AHG/Res.16(I), July 1964: respect for borders existing at independence.
